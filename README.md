## 🔬 Research & Projects

### 🎧 Scene Talk! — Multimodal Audio Description for Visually Impaired Users

**Hyundai AutoEver Barrier-Free Contest — Finalist (Top 7 Teams)** · `2026 – Ongoing`<br/>
`Vision-Language Models` `Video Understanding` `Accessibility`

**Problem** — Much of a video's story is carried by visuals alone. Visually impaired viewers miss it, and any description has to fit into the gaps between dialogue.

**Approach**
* **Product scope:** Generate context-aware audio descriptions by jointly analyzing audio, visual, and language modalities.
* **Where to speak:** Built a preprocessing pipeline combining VAD, FFmpeg scene detection, and STT to find non-dialogue intervals and reconstruct scene-level temporal context across long-form video.
* **What to look at:** Integrated Vision-Language Models with selective keyframe sampling, cutting redundant visual input and inference cost while preserving narrative coherence.

**Status** — Selected as a **finalist (Top 7 teams)**; building toward the final round.

<br/>

### 🎭 Preset-Based Patchwise Decomposition for 3D Avatar Expression Transfer

**Computer Graphics Lab, Ajou University** · `2026.03 – 2026.06`<br/>
`Computer Graphics` `3D Avatar` `Facial Animation` `Expression Transfer`

**Problem** — VRM/VRoid-style avatars come with a limited set of built-in expression presets, which limits how precisely a target expression can be reproduced.

**Approach**
* Decomposed built-in presets into semantic regions (mouth, eyes, brows, jaw) to construct localized deformation bases.
* Reconstructed target expressions as nonnegative least-squares combinations of patch-wise components.
* Designed synthetic ground-truth experiments using ARKit 52 blendshapes, comparing against preset-only selection and global preset mixing.

**Result** — Published and presented at **KCGS 2026 (First author, Oral Presentation)**.

### 🧱 Diffusion-based Unsupervised Crack Anomaly Detection

[![GitHub Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Project-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/KENTECH-AD-project/Infer_by_4_ways)

**ENVI Lab, KENTECH Winter Internship** · `2025.12 – 2026.04`<br/>
`Diffusion Models` `Anomaly Detection` `Structural Priors` `Computer Vision`

**Problem** — Diffusion-based reconstruction loses thin, linear structures during denoising, producing structural false positives in unsupervised anomaly detection.

**Approach**
* Designed a structure-aware pixel-wise weighted loss using **Structure Tensor** and **Frangi Filter** priors to preserve thin structures.
* Proposed **Diffusion-based Saliency Verification**, reinterpreting reconstruction residuals through structural priors and consistency checks.

**Result** — Published and presented at **JCCI 2026 (First author, Poster Presentation)**.

### 🤟 SIGN UP! — Real-time Sign Language Learning System

[![GitHub Project](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Project-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/GooChiSo)

**Google–Ajou University AI Capstone Design Competition** · `2025.09 – 2025.12`<br/>
`Temporal Modeling` `Human Motion` `Keypoint Processing` `HCI`

**Problem** — Learning sign language by imitating videos gives learners no feedback on whether their own motion is correct.

**Approach**
* **Product:** Designed an interaction where users learn through motion-based real-time feedback instead of passive video imitation.
* **Engineering:** Built a temporal preprocessing pipeline for hand keypoint streams that handles missing frames, unstable detections, and variable-length inputs for real-time inference.

**Result** — **Excellence Award**, ranked **5th out of 64 teams**.
